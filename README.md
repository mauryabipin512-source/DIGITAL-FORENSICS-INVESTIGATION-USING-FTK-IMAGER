## 1. Introduction

Digital Forensics is the process of collecting, preserving, examining, and analyzing digital evidence from computers, storage devices, and other digital media. The main objective of digital forensics is to investigate digital data while maintaining the integrity of the original evidence.

In this project, FTK Imager is used to acquire and analyze digital evidence. A forensic image of the selected evidence source is created and then examined to identify files, folders, metadata, deleted data, and other relevant digital artifacts.
## 2. Objectives

The main objectives of this Digital Forensics project are:

1. To understand the basic concept of Digital Forensics.
2. To acquire digital evidence using FTK Imager.
3. To create a forensic image of the selected evidence source.
4. To maintain and verify the integrity of digital evidence using hash values.
5. To analyze files and folders present in the forensic image.
6. To examine file metadata and timestamps.
7. To identify deleted files or data, if available.
8. To document the findings of the forensic investigation.
